常见问题 / FAQ

需要账号或 API key 吗？ / Do I need an account or API key?

不需要。开箱即用，转录、翻译、说话人识别、文本整理全部由 Apple 端上引擎在本机完成。云端引擎是可选的进阶功能，需要你自备 API key。
No. Everything works on-device with Apple's local engines out of the box. Cloud engines are an optional advanced feature where you bring your own API key.

数据存在哪里？ / Where is my data stored?

在你的设备本地；若你登录了 iCloud，还会同步到你个人的私有 iCloud。开发者无法访问。详见隐私政策。
On your device, and (if signed in) in your personal private iCloud. The developer has no access. See the Privacy Policy.

怎么录系统音频？ / How do I record system audio?

Mac：按提示授予「系统音频录制」权限，即可与麦克风同时录制 App 播放的声音。
iPhone / iPad：录音页展开「系统音频（进阶）」→ 点开始屏幕广播 → 在系统面板里选 Scritto。屏幕广播是 iOS 给第三方 App 的唯一通道（Apple 的隐私设计），因此过程中一定会有红色状态标识。FaceTime 与通话音频受系统保护，任何第三方 App 都抓不到。另外，外放播放的声音本来就会被麦克风一起收进去（戴耳机则收不到）。
Mac: grant the “System Audio Recording” permission when prompted; the app captures app playback alongside your microphone.
iPhone / iPad: on the recording screen open System audio (advanced), start a screen broadcast, and pick Scritto in the system panel. Screen broadcast is the only route iOS gives third-party apps, so a red recording indicator always appears. FaceTime and phone-call audio are protected by the system and cannot be captured by any third-party app. Sound played through the speaker is picked up by the microphone anyway (not with headphones on).

支持哪些语言？ / Which languages are supported?

转录与翻译的语言跟随 Apple 端上引擎的支持范围，按需在系统设置里下载；可选的云端引擎可以扩展覆盖。
Transcription and translation languages follow Apple's on-device engine support and download on demand in system settings. Optional cloud engines extend coverage.

在哪里找到 App 内的支持与隐私入口？ / Where are these links inside the app?

设置 → 通用 → 关于 →「支持与反馈」/「隐私政策」（Mac 与 iPhone 相同）。
Settings → General → About → “Support & Feedback” / “Privacy Policy” (same on Mac and iPhone).
