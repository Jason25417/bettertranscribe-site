Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期: 2026-07-10

English

Overview

BetterTranscribe is a local-first recording, transcription, and translation app for Mac and iPhone. It is designed so that everything works entirely on your device by default. We do not collect, store, or sell any of your data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers.

What stays on your device

By default, all of the following happens on-device using Apple frameworks, and never leaves your device: audio recording (microphone and system audio), transcription (Apple SpeechAnalyzer), translation (Apple Translation), speaker identification (on-device models), and text polishing (Apple on-device intelligence models). Your recordings and transcripts are stored locally on your device.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your session library syncs through your personal, private iCloud database (CloudKit). This data is protected by your Apple Account and is not accessible to the developer or anyone else.

Optional cloud engines (off by default)

You may optionally configure third-party cloud engines using your own API keys (for example: Deepgram for transcription, pyannoteAI for speaker identification, or LLM providers for translation). These are off by default and only used when you explicitly enable them.

  • When you enable a cloud engine, the relevant audio or text is sent directly from your device to that provider to perform the task you requested, under that provider's own privacy policy.
  • Your API keys are stored only in the system Keychain on your device.
  • The developer never receives, proxies, or has access to this data.

Calendar access (optional)

If you grant calendar access, the app reads event names for the time of your recording, only to suggest a session name. This information stays on your device.

Data retention and deletion

All data is under your control. You can delete any session, transcript, or voiceprint inside the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all local data; iCloud data can be managed in your device's iCloud settings.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.

中文

概述

BetterTranscribe 是一款本地优先的录音、转录与翻译 App（Mac / iPhone）。默认状态下一切功能完全在你的设备上完成。开发者不收集、不存储、不出售你的任何数据。App 不含统计分析、广告和任何追踪器。

留在设备上的内容

默认情况下，以下处理全部通过 Apple 框架在设备端完成，数据不离开设备：录音（麦克风与系统音频）、转录（Apple SpeechAnalyzer）、翻译（Apple Translation）、说话人识别（端上模型）、文本整理（Apple 端上智能模型）。录音与文稿存储在你的设备本地。

iCloud 同步

若你登录了 iCloud，会话库通过你个人的私有 iCloud 数据库（CloudKit）同步。这些数据受你的 Apple 账户保护，开发者与任何第三方均无法访问。

可选云端引擎（默认关闭）

你可以选择用自己的 API key 配置第三方云端引擎（如 Deepgram 转录、pyannoteAI 声纹识别、LLM 服务商翻译）。它们默认关闭，仅在你显式启用后使用。

  • 启用后，相关音频或文本会从你的设备直接发送给该服务商以完成你请求的任务，适用其自身隐私政策。
  • API key 仅保存在你设备的系统钥匙串中。
  • 开发者不经手、不代理、也无法访问这些数据。

日历权限（可选）

若你授权日历，App 仅读取录音时段对应的事件名，用于推荐会话名称。该信息不离开设备。

数据保留与删除

所有数据由你掌控。可随时在 App 内删除任何会话、文稿或声纹。删除 App 即清除全部本地数据；iCloud 数据可在系统 iCloud 设置中管理。

儿童

本 App 不向任何人（包括儿童）收集数据。

变更

政策如有变更，将在本页更新并标注新的生效日期。
